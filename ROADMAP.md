## 📌 Standard Format Used for Every Lesson:
- **A. Topic:** Roadmap location & context
- **B. Simple Explanation:** Beginner-friendly explanation
- **C. Why Do We Need It?** Practical problem solved
- **D. Syntax:** Clear syntax breakdown
- **E. Basic Example:** Annotated code
- **F. Expected Output:** What appears and why
- **G. Real-World Usage:** Applied scenario in actual software
- **H. Common Mistakes:** Gotchas & error messages
- **I. Practice:** 2 beginner, 1 intermediate, 1 real-world challenge
- **J. Lesson Recap:** Key takeaways
- **K. Progress:** Tracking next steps
